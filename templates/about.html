About JobBoard

JobBoard is a web portal where you can browse and search job vacancies, and (after logging in) add your own posts.

Guest (without login)

  • Browse the list of vacancies
  • Search by title/company/description, location, and category
  • View this About page

Logged-in user

  • Add a new job vacancy
  • Edit or delete your own vacancies
  • Update your profile (name, email, photo)
